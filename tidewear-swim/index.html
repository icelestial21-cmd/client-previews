FAQ

Good questions

Still unsure? Message us and a real person will reply.

How does print on demand work?

Every suit is printed and sewn after you order, so there's no overstock and every print is possible.

Can I use my own print?

Yes. Upload an image in the design studio and preview it on the suit in 3D. Seamless patterns and high-resolution photos work best.

Will the print fade?

Sublimation dyes the fibre itself, so prints stay bright through sun, salt and chlorine.

What if the fit isn't right?

Check the size guide on each suit. If there's a printing or sewing fault, we'll remake it free within 30 days.
