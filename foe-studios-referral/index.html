The rules, in plain language

Simple terms. No fine print surprises.

Suggested terms for FOE to review and confirm before launch.

  1. First introduction countsThe ambassador whose link or form brings the brand in first is credited.
  2. A 90-day windowA referral stays attributed to you for 90 days from the first click or form (window for FOE to confirm).
  3. Paid on completionRewards are released when the referred project is completed, not when it's signed.
  4. One-time project feesRewards apply to the project fee, not ongoing monthly retainers.
  5. Honest referrals onlyRefer people who know you're introducing them. No self-referrals.
Who can become an ambassador?

Clients, partners, friends of the studio: anyone who knows a business, organization or individual that needs strategy, branding or content.

Do I need to sell anything?

No. Make the introduction. FOE Studios handles the discovery call, proposal and the work itself.

How do I track my referrals?

Your ambassador dashboard shows clicks, referrals, projects in progress and rewards released.

What if the project is cancelled?

Rewards are paid on completion, so a project cancelled before completion doesn't earn a reward.

How much is the reward?

Reward amounts and tier levels will be set by FOE Studios and published here before launch.
