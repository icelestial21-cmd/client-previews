FAQ

Good questions

Still unsure? Message us and a real person will reply.

How does print on demand work?

Nothing is printed until you order. Your tee is printed one at a time by our print partner, then shipped straight to you, so there's no waste and no minimums.

Can I upload my own design?

Yes. Upload a PNG, JPG or SVG in the design studio and see it on the tee in 3D before you buy. A transparent PNG at 3000 px prints best.

How long does it take?

Printing takes 2–4 business days, then shipping time depends on where you are. You get tracking as soon as it ships.

What if there's a printing fault?

If your tee arrives misprinted or damaged, send us a photo within 30 days and we'll reprint it free.
