The Caribbean Sports Quest platform, rebuilt as a phone app with Quest Points running through every screen. Open this link on your phone and tap “Add to Home Screen” to use it like a real app.

  • Home your Quest Points, what's on this week, the daily quiz and the Weekly Pulse
  • Events by sport and island, tickets in JMD and USD, “I'm going”, live scores and check-in
  • Leagues Leagues & Tournaments with tables, fixtures and results
  • Rewards the Reward Vault, weekly fan league, CSQ Store and partner-store reservations
  • Me tickets with QR codes, Quest code, rank, badges, streak and alerts
  • Also sports travel (stays and trails), Sports Opportunities and the Locker Room

Everything you tap works, and it remembers you on this device. Events, teams, scores, prices and posts are sample data; no payment is taken.
