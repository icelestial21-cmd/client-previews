THE PHILOSOPHY OF THE SHEARS

Chibest Fashion Worldwide was born out of an uncompromising refusal to treat African ceremonial dress as secondary to European tailoring. In our Pantang atelier, we apply the exact structural rigor of Savile Row—chest canvas haircloth roping, hand-basted shoulder heads, and balanced drape lines—to the grand majestic proportions of West African royal robes.

While traditional African tailoring has historically required in-person fittings, our computer-vision anthropometric engine bridges the transcontinental gap. Whether you are walking into a state banquet in Mayfair, a boardroom in Manhattan, or a traditional wedding in Lagos, your Chibest garment fits with absolute mathematical certainty.

THE FOUR PILLARS OF OUR ART

01 · EUROPEAN TEXTILE SOURCING

Super 140s & Royal Damask

We import directly from historical mills in Biella, Italy and Huddersfield, England. High-twist virgin wools designed to hold a razor crease in tropical humidity.

02 · INDIVIDUAL PATTERN SHEARING

Zero Pre-Made Templates

Every client pattern is struck directly in chalk by Master Chibest on the cutting table from your millimeter coordinates. No mass-market slopers or grading.

03 · FREEHAND BULLION EMBROIDERY

60+ Hours by Hand

Our embroidery masters guide heavy metallic gold threads entirely freehand across the fabric, weaving complex ancestral geometry into every tunic and cape.

04 · TRANSCONTINENTAL DISPATCH

3–5 Days via DHL Express

Carefully packaged in bespoke garment bags and boxed for direct courier air freight to your residence in the UK, North America, or Europe.
