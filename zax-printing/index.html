About us

Learning, printing
and celebrations.

Z.A.X Charts and More Events Planning and Printing is a creative business dedicated to providing high-quality, affordable, and personalised educational materials, printing services, and event-planning solutions.

Our mission

Our mission is to bring creativity, learning, and celebrations to life through beautifully designed, affordable, and customised products. We aim to support students, parents, teachers, schools, individuals, and businesses by turning their ideas into meaningful creations.

Areas we serve

We are based in Cave Valley, Hanover, Jamaica, and serve customers throughout Hanover and St. Ann, with arrangements available for customers in other parts of Jamaica.
