Help

How a day with your admin panel works.

  1. A new order comes inOrders from your website land in Orders as "New". Take a phone or walk-in order with New order.
  2. Price it and send the quoteOpen the order, type the price, then send the quote from Confirmations. The order moves to "Quoted".
  3. Deposit receivedSwitch on "Deposit received". The order is confirmed and a confirmation message is ready to send.
  4. Make it, then mark it readyMove it to "In progress", then "Ready". A pick-up message with the balance is ready to send.
  5. DoneMark it "Completed" and send a thank-you. Change photos, services and details any time under Settings.
