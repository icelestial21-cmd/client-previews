Master Trade Specializations

Comprehensive Civil & Residential Trades

MMS Construction Services eliminates the headache of coordinating multiple sub-contractors. We provide end-to-end structural execution from ground-breaking to luxury handover.

House Belting & Suspended Decking

Monolithic reinforced concrete belt beams (ring beams) and suspended concrete roof slabs engineered to resist Category 5 Caribbean hurricanes and seismic tremors.

  • Formwork carpentry & propping to zero-sag tolerance
  • High-slump vibrated concrete pour for zero honeycombing
  • Level screed finish for direct waterproofing & drainage

Steeling & Rebar Structural Caging

Precision rebar cutting, bending, and tying for foundation footings, vertical column cages, cantilever beams, and multi-story structural continuity.

  • High-yield deformed steel bars (1/2", 5/8", 3/4")
  • Strict tie-wire spacing per architectural structural drawings
  • Starter bar extensions for future upper-floor additions

Home Renovations & Second-Floor Additions

Expanding existing Jamaican residences with additional bedrooms, verandas, self-contained rental suites, and structural doorway remodeling.

  • Load-bearing wall removal with steel lintel insertion
  • Solid concrete block masonry with core-fill grouting
  • Seamless integration with existing rooflines & plumbing

Luxury Italian Marble & Porcelain Tiling

Laser-aligned wall and floor tiling using European clip-and-wedge leveling systems. Specializing in large-format slabs, mosaic columns, and curbless walk-in showers.

  • 360° Green laser level calibration for zero lippage
  • High-traffic epoxy and polymer-fortified grouting
  • Custom niches, wrapped architectural columns, and feature walls

Roof Screeding & Membrane Waterproofing

Preventing moisture ingress and slab degradation across Jamaican concrete roofs. We repair ponding water, apply torch-on membranes, and build weather-proof parapets.

  • Slope-correcting mortar screed to scuppers and downpipes
  • Multi-layer elastomeric and bitumen waterproofing coats
  • Pitched timber trusses with standing-seam Aluzinc sheets

Rough-In Plumbing, Electrical & Painting

Complete MEP infrastructure roughed into concrete masonry before plastering. Clean conduit runs, distribution panel boards, water tanks, and airless spray painting.

  • Concealed PVC and CPVC hot/cold water distribution
  • Gravity-fed and pressure-pump water tank installations
  • Premium weather-shield exterior and silk-matte interior paint
