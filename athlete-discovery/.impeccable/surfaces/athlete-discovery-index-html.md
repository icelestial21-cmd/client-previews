## Direction contract

THESIS: Apex is the season's meet programme. Every athlete is an entry on a heat sheet with a place, a mark and an official stamp, so the talent reads as on-the-record, not marketed. It refuses the category's broadcast dashboard: navy chrome, stat cards, glowing ticker.

OWN-WORLD: Cool newsprint ground (grey-white, never cream), black ink, one spot red for stamps and the primary action, blue ballpoint for the user's own marks. Condensed grotesque for event headers and names; tabular figures for marks. Ruled heat-sheet tables, stamped verification (ID CHECKED, RESULTS CHECKED, MEASURED AT COMBINE), halftone duotone photos. Dark mode is the floodlit night session: slate paper, white ink, same red.

STORY: The visitor reads a programme already full of real-looking entries, sees each figure stamped with how it was checked, and searches it for the athletes they came for.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Programme masthead and running header (nav). Left two thirds: the headline set like the meet title, one-line offer, then "Find an athlete" search with sport chips, then the top-five heat sheet with places, marks and stamps. Right third: the featured entry's halftone photo with its stamped grade. Results-in-brief column below, printed, not scrolling.

FORM: Schools-athletics meet programme and heat sheets; position 1 on my ordered list (Impeccable's pick); seed key e83fea17. Signature interaction: the blue ballpoint circle drawn around an entry when it is shortlisted or compared; the stamp pressing in when something is verified or signed. One authored motion moment, reduced-motion safe.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
