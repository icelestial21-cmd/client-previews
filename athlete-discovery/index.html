            What can the agent do on the athlete’s behalf?

            1. Term. 24 months from the date both parties sign. Either party may end the agreement for any reason with 30 days’ written notice, or immediately if the other party materially breaches it.

            2. Commission. The agent is paid only a percentage of money the athlete earns from contracts and sponsorship the agent negotiates. No upfront or retainer fees. No commission on scholarships.

            3. Data. The athlete owns their profile, results and medical information, and decides who can see them.

            4. Payments. Contract and sponsor payments are held by a licensed escrow partner and released when both parties confirm.

            5. Minors. If the athlete is under 18, a parent or legal guardian must sign as well.

            6. Governing law. The laws of Jamaica. Disputes go to binding arbitration in Kingston, Jamaica.

            Sample clauses for the demo only. Not legal advice; a live agreement must be reviewed by counsel in each country.
