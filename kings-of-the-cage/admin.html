How the countdown works

Before the event it shows the days to go, then "Tomorrow" and "Today". After the event it reads "Played · results soon" until you point the bar at the next one.

Help

How the admin page fits into a Kings of the Cage season.

  1. Point the announcement bar at the next eventSet the name, venue and date under Announcement bar. The countdown runs on every page.
  2. Teams registerRegistrations arrive in Teams as "New". Check the roster, then mark the team Confirmed and send the captain the confirmation on WhatsApp.
  3. Fees in, spots filledSwitch on "Fee paid" when the fee arrives. When the field is full, new teams go to the waitlist.
  4. Sponsors come on boardMove each enquiry from New to In talks, Proposal sent and Signed. Signed lines show as claimed on the budget board.
  5. After Finals NightAdd the winner and numbers under Editions & results, upload the photos to the Gallery, and point the bar at the next one.
