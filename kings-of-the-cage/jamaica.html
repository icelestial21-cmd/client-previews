Everything players, fans and partners usually ask first.

Ask the organisers
What is Kings of the Cage?

A 4v4 small-goal football tournament founded by Renaldo Alexander in Maloney Gardens, Trinidad. It started in 2020 and now runs every summer, with one-day editions in Tobago, Brooklyn and, next, Jamaica.

Why 4v4 small goal?

It's fast-paced and rewards quick thinking, skill and execution. Every possession matters, which is why the format pulls in players and crowds of all ages.

How long does a tournament run?

The main summer edition runs six to eight weeks of weekend fixtures, ending with Finals Night. The travelling editions (Tobago, Christmas Ball, New York and Jamaica) are played in one or two days.

How does a team enter?

Registration opens before each edition, and places are limited: in 2026, 32 teams wanted in and 22 got a spot. For Kings of the Cage Jamaica, register your interest on the Jamaica page and we'll confirm fees and dates.

Can I watch if I can't be there?

Yes. Matches stream live on Instagram, and fans from North America, Europe, South America, Asia, Australia and the Caribbean have tuned in.

How can my brand get involved?

Title, supporting and night partners are welcome. Hennessy held naming rights to All Star Night in 2026, with Gatorade as official hydration. See the Jamaica sponsor packages, or get in touch.
