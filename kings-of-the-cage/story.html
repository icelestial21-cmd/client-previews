From Maloney to the world

Kings of the Cage started on an outdoor court in Maloney Gardens in the summer of 2020. Six summers later it holds the indoor attendance record for sport in Trinidad & Tobago and has been played in Tobago and Brooklyn.

Firsts

Things nobody had done before

Championship Rings

2024: the first sporting event in the country to award rings to its champions.

All Star Night

2025: the first All Star format at a football tournament in Trinidad & Tobago.

A real cage

2024: a full cage on the court, so Kings of the Cage is exactly that.

Record crowd

2025: 1,300+ at the finals, the most for an indoor sporting event in T&T.

202031 Jul – 30 Aug · Maloney Outdoor Court

KOC I · The first edition

The community came out in full, and visitors from outside Maloney were wowed by the welcome. COVID-19 restrictions ended the tournament before the final, but it had already made its mark.

National footballer Kenwyne Jones and soca artistes Lyrikal Voice, Bunji Garlin and Fayann Lyons-Alvarez endorsed it. An ad ran on Boom Champions 94.1, clips showed on TTT and it made the back page of the Sunday Newsday.

KOC II · The restart

Rebuilt from scratch and moved indoors. A knockout phase on opening night carried its own TTD 3,000 prize.

The Instagram live stream reached America, England and Asia, and the tournament was featured five times on TTT sports news, with a month of ads on 94.1.

202314 Jul – 20 Aug · six weeks

KOC III · Fifty grand

The grand prize doubled to TTD 50,000, won by Jesus Youths of Jonestown, Arima. The live stream added Australia to its audience.

Kids' nights brought free popcorn, hot dogs, snow cones, ice cream, lollies and cotton candy, all made on the spot. Finals night was praised for its international standard.

202316–17 Dec · Shaw Park, Tobago

Kings of the Cage Tobago

The first time the tournament left Trinidad: one team from Tobago and three from Trinidad at the Shaw Park Outdoor Facility. Ricochae of Trinidad took the TTD 20,000 prize.

Five days later, on 22 December, the one-day Christmas Ball returned to the Maloney Outdoor Court: six teams, TTD 10,000, won by 1 Law.

202419 Jul – 25 Aug

KOC IV · The Cage arrives

More than 1,200 people came to the finals, the highest attendance for an indoor sporting event in Trinidad & Tobago at the time. A real cage went up on court, bringing the name to life.

On Back to School night, patrons came in school uniform. Three boys and three girls received school bags and supplies, and grocery bags went out to patrons. Squad Elite of Maloney Gardens won.

20254 Jul – 17 Aug
Record year

KOC V · The Milestone

More than 1,300 at the finals set a new record for indoor sport in the country, with a presentation built to feel like the Bernabéu or San Siro.

All Star Night arrived: 18 standout players split into two teams of nine for All Star challenges and a game, a first for a football tournament in Trinidad & Tobago.

Kings of the Cage New York

Over Thanksgiving weekend, the tournament went to Brooklyn's Gary Owens Community Center, home of the largest indoor turf in New York City. The crowd turned out in full.

The second New York leg returns to Brooklyn on Saturday 21 November 2026.

202610 Jul – 30 Aug · eight weeks
Champions: Squad Elite · 3-peat

KOC VI · The three-peat

Demand hit 32 teams and 22 made the cut. Instagram and TikTok passed 1 million views again, with fans watching across North America, Europe, South America and the Caribbean.

Finals Night brought an aerialist in the roof, a red-carpet walkout and cold sparks. Squad Elite won a third straight title, and All Star Night returned with Hennessy holding naming rights and Gatorade as official hydration.

Where it started

August 2020: the back page

The first flyer promised TTD 20,000 in cash and prizes at the Maloney Outdoor Court. Within weeks the tournament was on the back page of the Sunday Newsday, even though COVID-19 meant it never reached a final.

Five years later, the same idea fills an arena.
